## Excluded from the public repository

Listed with hashes in `docs/excluded-files-manifest.json` (77 files, 46 MB) so a local copy can be verified:

- Page renders and crops from *Corpus of Indus Seals and Inscriptions* vol. 1 (Joshi & Parpola 1987) and vol. 2 (Shah & Parpola 1991), Parpola 1994 and 2011, Gadd 1932, Mahadevan 1977.
- Photographs from harappa.com.
- The Indus sign webfont from yajnadevam/lipi.
- Application source of Tejesh Raut's explorer and of yajnadevam/lipi (`index.vue`). `indus-lab/round46-brahmi-coverage/fetch_sources.sh` re-downloads them.

Full CISI PDFs and the Kuiper OCR text were never bundled. URLs are in `indus-lab/sources.json`, `docs/EXTERNAL-EVIDENCE.md` and the per-round provenance files.
