## Data facts you need
- Corpus: `indus-lab/raw/inscriptions.csv`, 5,679 rows, SHA256 `c22d39c3…3429`, text in column index 34. Core text types = 1,936.
  - Loader: `round30/foreign_context.py` → `FC.TRAIN` (sorted core types, **reading order**, sign IDs as 3-digit strings).
  - 582 sign types; ~8,700 tokens; ~15 tokens/sign.
- Copper-tablet texts in `round24/predictions.json` are stored **reversed** relative to `FC.TRAIN`.
- Sign IDs are catalogue IDs, not values. Numeral stroke signs: 001/031 = one, 002/032 = two, 003/013/033, 004/014/034, 005/015/035, 016/036, 017, 018. Pictographs used as anchors: 220 fish, 090 man, 520 arrow, 740 jar, 803/806 leaf, 269 bird.
- Proto-Dravidian: `indus-lab/external/dravidian_db_v1.1/pdr_04.csv` (Zenodo 22215802, CC BY 4.0). Prefer Krishnamurti 2003 rows. The `simplified` column drops long vowels, so use `round37/fingerprint.py:norm` on `reconstruction` instead.
- Shared model code:
  - `round41/anchored.py`: char-trigram LM, annealing `fit`, `heldout`, `split` with near-duplicate removal, `permute`.
  - `round37/fingerprint.py`: `norm`, loaders.
  - `round32/benchmark.py`: Linear B.
- Gotchas:
  - `LM.cache` grows without bound. Call `lm.cache.clear()` per fit (round 43 fix) or long runs get OOM-killed.
  - `python3 -I` breaks the sibling imports. Run scripts normally from their round folder.
  - Each fit (60k steps) takes ~4 s; 2 CPUs ≈ 1,000 fits in 35 min.
- Gulf/Cuneiform: derived CDLI extracts are in `data/derived/` (Dilmun/Meluhha lines). The full dump is `cdli-gh/data` (see `setup --all`). The name pool is in `round35/names.tsv`.
- `data/derived/kuiper1991_ch5_words.txt`: the 386 parsed Kuiper foreign words (the script reparses them from `data/kuiper.txt`).

## What is established (survived controls)
1. **Round 30–31:** Gulf/Mesopotamian round seals are statistically foreign in sign order (percentile 0.916, p<5e-5). This is robust to transcription doubts. It cannot tell a foreign language from names or pseudo-script.
2. **Round 32:** at Indus corpus size (~15 tokens/sign), statistics alone cannot recover sound values. Linear B collapses between 20 and 14 tokens/sign. About 40 anchored signs would be needed.
3. **Rounds 39–40:** meaning-constrained matching of Kuiper's Rigvedic foreign words leans Proto-Dravidian over Munda and over controls, and it is not a dictionary-size artefact. It is below the attribution bar. This is the only positive language signal, and it concerns the substrate, not the script.
4. **Round 27/29:** sign order transfers between cities. The script has real structure (bigram grammar), but that says nothing about sound.

## What failed or is parked (don't redo without a new idea)
- Rebus tournament (36), substrate fingerprint (37), sound-only matching (38): their controls failed.
- Gulf seals vs attested names (35): zero power.
- **Anchor-rebus branch, rounds 41–45: PARKED.**
  - The Dravidian anchors beat ~95% of shuffles (p≈0.06), but **Finnish anchors beat them more strongly (p=0.016)**. The method rewards word shape, not the true language.
  - The round 44 copper-tablet protocol exists but must not be run on this basis.
- Tamil pattern matching (20), correspondence decoders (22–23), copper-tablet labels (24–26): weak or null.
