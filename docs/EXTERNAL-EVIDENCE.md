# External evidence investigated after round28

Research notes as of 11 October 2026. URLs are traceable leads; no full external archive is bundled. This section records conclusions from reading sources, not new discoveries or completed phonetic tests.

## Meluhhan-associated names
- Nisaba 15, 371 / CDLI P453801: https://cdli.earth/artifacts/453801
- Older transcription revision: https://cdli.earth/inscriptions/2249158
- CUSAS 40, 1354 / P516138: https://cdli.earth/artifacts/516138
- CUSAS 40, 1582 / P516366: https://cdli.earth/artifacts/516366

These Sumerian administrative records list oil allocations to Nanasa, Samar and Ali'ahi and associate the group with Meluhha. Current CDLI transliterations use na-na-sa3; the older revision has na-na-za. Do not pick a spelling to suit a proposed etymology. CUSAS1354's year six is bracketed/restored in the book index, although CDLI metadata presents year six; month three. The two other entries have year six/month twelve. P453801 and P516366 have very similar texts and the same date: physical duplication remains unresolved, NOT demonstrated. No public object photos were available on the CDLI entries checked.

Being called Meluhhan does not prove the individual's name is native Meluhhan, still less Dravidian. Ali'ahi is discussed as Akkadian in the 2025 research article. No Indus spelling of any name has been linked to these records. A scribe's seal on a ration document need not belong to the recipients. The fragmentary CUSAS1354 seal inscription does not supply a demonstrated Indus connection.

## Ur seal identity: confirmed, phonetic linkage absent
British Museum: https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/W_1932-1008-178
CSMVS record: https://networksofthepast.csmvs.in/object/seal-and-sealing/
Gadd original study: https://ignca.gov.in/Asi_data/33779.pdf
Gadd printed pp13–14, PDF zero-based pages18–19 were visually inspected.

Identity: Gadd No16 = U.17649 = BM123208 = registration1932,1008.178.
Context: fill of grave shaft PG1847, Ur. Does not identify an owner or link to the Meluhhan ration recipients.
Existing dataset row3898.1: excavation-idno U17649; text +002-004-328-001-803-415+; direction R/L; type SEAL:C; physical preservation marked complete.
Gadd describes chipping but treats the inscription as complete. Museum records chipping and raises possible pseudo-inscription status. The original CSV has not been silently corrected: use an explicit annotation layer. The six sign IDs have NOT been independently validated against a photograph in this branch. This seal must not be used as a confirmed sound-value anchor.

## Avoid overstating the elephant-word lead
See indus-lab/round21/external_word_audit.md. Published Proto-Dravidian *pal 'tooth' is different from *paal 'milk'. The proposed route to elephant words is a hypothesis, not an attested Meluhhan lexeme. Hurrian ivory terminology discussed in Speiser includes an Akkadian loan and is not independent testimony for a Meluhhan donor.
