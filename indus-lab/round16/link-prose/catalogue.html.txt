How the scores were set Importance asks what a full reading would add: 5 for a first-hand report of a major event by a principal witness (du Bellay in London 1529, Lanssac at the Polish election), 2 for routine business. Solvability asks whether the material online is enough: 5 when deciphered siblings of the same hand and year sit in the same volume, 1 for one short letter in an unknown language. Difficulty is the technical work at the keyboard: 1 for aligning a known sibling, 5 for a statistics-only attack on a large homophonic nomenclator. The default priority weights importance 0.4, solvability 0.35 and ease 0.25 (ease is 6 minus difficulty), rescaled to 0–10; move the sliders to see the order change. Every score is a judgement made before viewing the leaf, so treat the order as a work plan, not a finding.
