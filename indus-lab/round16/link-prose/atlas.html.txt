About the data Each arc is one document from a target's profile.json , the record kept for every cipher on this site. It is placed at the city where the letter was written and the city it was sent to, as the notes state them. A court or a person is placed where they were at that date. Letters whose sender or recipient cannot be located from the evidence are left off rather than guessed. So are the cryptograms with no route at all, such as Voynich, Scorpion and the gold bars. The coastlines are modern (Natural Earth); the map draws no frontiers, because they moved too often over five centuries. Colour gives the outcome of the write-up: read , explained or found already read , read in part , attempted .
