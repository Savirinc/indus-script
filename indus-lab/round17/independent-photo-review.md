### M-593 A

The row has a convincing broad ordered resemblance to M-534 A: complex upright/crossbar groups toward the left, a branched element near a central enclosure, a group of short parallel uprights, and a U-like form toward the right. This is more than a resemblance based on one isolated line: several shapes appear in comparable successive positions.

The central enclosed form looks more angular or diamond-like than M-534's rounded, overlapping outlines. Its internal marks are not resolved well enough to establish exact equivalence. The left-hand groups are less distinct, and the far-right area is affected by bright mottling and weak boundaries. I cannot confidently match every projection of the terminal form to M-534. The exact number and membership of the nearby upright strokes also remain uncertain.

The difference in central enclosure shape could reflect engraving variation, depiction/contrast, damage, or a genuine structural difference. This photograph alone does not decide among those possibilities.

### M-1547 A

This face is substantially harder to read. A strong dark diagonal break runs down from the upper edge through the region of the faint line. I exclude that irregular broad feature from the candidate engraved sequence.

There are scattered visible upright, curved, and angular traces across the lower-middle portion. A small cluster of short uprights right of center is relatively noticeable, with weaker curved or angular marks nearby. Much of the left and central detail is low contrast, interrupted, or mixed with surface texture. I cannot trace a secure, continuous full sequence with reliable unit boundaries.

Some simple features are compatible with portions of the other two rows, but that is insufficient to establish a position-by-position match. A full matching sequence cannot be recovered simply by filling the weak areas from the clearer photographs.
