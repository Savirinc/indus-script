## Open constraints and disciplined next use

- **Meaning remains unanchored.** Names, heads, suffixes, words and referents are frequently asserted after positional or association tests. Their operational definitions can be useful, but the language-based interpretation requires an external semantic anchor.
- **The strongest useful lead is graphic structure:** cages, internal strokes, fish variants, terminal signs and count-like sequences have reported reproducible distributional differences. These are candidates for photo-grounded validation with conservative labels, not preassigned meanings.
- **Duplicates remain an archaeological question.** Text-based grouping approximates mould identity. Different moulds may share text; differently damaged readings may hide one mould; separately carved pieces can still share production or reuse history. The author's later distinction between handmade and moulded observations does not itself verify independence.
- **Chronology needs the earlier object-level audit.** Sets 196 and 228 use ICIT early/late labels including early 1–3B and late 3C onward. This reading task did not inspect their code or original dating sources. Do not turn the claimed cross-period consistency into established continuity without date-scheme and object-type review.
- **The repeatedly reused A/B checks are not forever-unseen validation.** Hundreds of adaptive model/rule/configuration decisions use the same pools. A newly chosen random split of already explored objects is useful internal sensitivity analysis, but fresh objects, held-out production groups and frozen analysis remain stronger confirmation.
- **FDR estimates need dependence and selection scrutiny.** Mean shuffled unit count divided by real unit count is the author's operational estimate; it is not a guarantee for each chosen label. Interdependent n-grams, repeatedly tuned thresholds, post-hoc picture categories and limited shuffle counts can matter. Set 345 acknowledges threshold-selection noise directly.
- **Known-script controls need expansion.** Passing two previously successful words cannot demonstrate that every extension finds semantic words rather than contextual associations. The free-fitting failure on Linear B is particularly valuable as a rejection test.
- **Restoration requires identity and visual checks.** M77 alignment can identify another object with the same frame; shared transcriptions and completed training texts cause leakage. Freeze predictions, remove all relevant copies/near-copies, compare to a contextual baseline, then have images independently annotated.
- **Held totals are not a success percentage.** The cumulative tallies preserve some subsequently withdrawn nominal passes and mix substantive hypotheses, adoption rules, bookkeeping gains and deliberately failing non-progress rules. The latest visible tally, 1,581 held/1,492 failed across 3,073 registered, is a ledger count, not confidence in a decipherment.
- **Pending work is concrete:** set 350 has only its registration. Separately, more informative evidence would be production-aware image matching, better gap photographs and genuinely external validation of the corrected picture-association configuration. This audit has not executed any of those steps.
