# Round 12 — discriminating experiment and identifiability gate

Independent methods review; 2026-10-10. Read round 11 protocols, code, strict cross-face results and photographic review. This document proposes experiments; no inferential result is claimed here.

## Decision

The current face-text data alone cannot distinguish a quantity interpretation from an unrestricted category interpretation. A category model can name its categories one-stroke, two-stroke, etc. and reproduce every observed pairing. More computation or agreement among agents does not break that equivalence.

The strongest feasible internal experiment is **cross-face stroke-count concordance**, followed by an independent magnitude-linkage search. A positive internal result would establish an ordered structural relation worth explaining, not amounts, commodity, arithmetic, language or sounds.

## What is already established

Round 11 retrieves 27 selected variable-context families, 120 target-face rows, and 119 ID stems, all at Harappa. They are selected for variation and cannot serve as an unbiased denominator. The H-315/316/317 photographs support a broadly similar other-face arrangement paired with two, three and four long grooves alongside the U-like form. H-316/H-1304 is a possible duplicate; H-355 has repeated target-face rows. Retain the full face multiset, not a set that discards repeated faces.

Exploratory feasibility audit this round: 64 ID stems in the complete raw CSV have both (a) a two-token face containing 700 and a code031–038 and (b) at least one code001–007 somewhere on another supplied row. This includes incomplete/uncertain rows and possible aliases, so **64 is not a final sample size**. It is enough to justify constructing an eligibility ledger, not a significance claim.

In the119 round11 stems, 112 have nonzero horizontal dimensions,110 vertical,17 thickness;99 have material labels. These are dimensions of the inscribed object, not the amount of an associated commodity. Their correlation with stroke count cannot establish quantity: the marks themselves occupy space, and workshops or object classes may differ in size. Stratigraphic/context fields have missing and differently formatted values; they require normalization before use as controls.

## Experiment A: visible counts on linked faces

1. Start from **all** raw objects with linked faces, not the27 variable families. Preserve source row/face IDs, full face lists, catalogue aliases and unknowns. Produce an inclusion/exclusion ledger before seeing the concordance result.
2. Target a simple700+long-stroke face, with exactly one short-stroke group on another complete legible face. Do not flatten lines, pool short and long forms, or infer stroke count by subtracting catalogue numbers. Obtain count mappings from inspected shapes, and photographically validate analysis examples with independent annotations.
3. Predeclare treatment of multiple short groups: exclude from the primary one-to-one test and retain for a separate inventory. Do not choose a convenient group after observing its value. Multiple repeated target faces belong to one object; discordant target values are separate flagged cases.
4. Preserve the rest of the other-face text as a template with its short-stroke group masked. Group by this template, site and reliable object class. Compare only groups having variation on both sides. Report the number of independent object/production families and distinct configurations, not just copies.
5. Primary statistic: within-template ordered concordance between independently observed long and short stroke counts (for example a weighted concordant-minus-discordant pair statistic, equal weight per template). This tests association in literal counts. Do not silently make literal equality the expected rule.
6. Report literal equal-count frequency separately as a prespecified descriptive hypothesis. An equality relation is narrower than quantity generally; failure rejects neither quantity nor numerical notation generally.
7. Fit only prespecified simple ordinal and nominal alternatives if the eligible sample permits: intercept/template-only, one global ordered count effect, and a regularized nominal-code model. Grouped cross-validation must keep aliases, identical full-object configurations, and known production/mould families together. Hold out full templates for transportability, with an explicit no-prediction outcome where training provides no support.

### Controls

- Permute long-count labels at object-family level within valid template/site/type strata. Preserve missingness and observed margins. Tiny or constant strata are not evidence and may give no possible nontrivial permutations.
- Randomly permute the short-series code-to-count mapping globally, preserving each code's occurrence frequency. This asks whether visible order performs better than arbitrary relabelling; it does not prove what that order meant.
- Use unrelated cross-face sign classes matched as far as possible on frequency/support as a descriptive negative control, chosen before the target result. Do not select the weakest negative after testing many.
- Repeat with one representative per exact full-object configuration and with conservative alias merges. Show sensitivity to material/type and documented production-family restrictions. Repeated objects from one mould do not create independent replications.
- Report excluded/uncertain photographs and every counterexample. Do not impute damaged counts from the pattern being tested.

### Recoverability before interpreting a null

Simulate on the exact eligible object/template structure, keeping missingness and clustering: (i) independent categorical labels; (ii) a known positive ordered relation with moderate error; (iii) an association driven only by workshop/template; and (iv) duplicated copies of a few formulas. Run the complete selection, splitting and test pipeline on these simulations. Report false-positive and detection rates with simulation uncertainty. If a realistically noisy ordered relation cannot be recovered at the available sample size, the corpus test is underpowered and a null is inconclusive. Synthetic successful recovery validates a statistical procedure, not archaeological assumptions.

### Holdout honesty

The corpus and target family have already been inspected. A new grouped split is useful for checking model overfitting but is **not genuinely unseen archaeological confirmation**. Independent photo annotations, an independently edited transcription, or subsequently acquired excavation objects provide stronger confirmation. Freeze the protocol and source hash before running results, but label the study exploratory because the hypothesis was data-informed.

## Experiment B: evidence that could actually discriminate semantics

Seek a securely associated, independently measured referent: contents counted in a sealed container, a calibrated weight/capacity association, or explicit multiple fields whose quantities demonstrably sum to an independently identified total across repeated cases. The inscribed tablet's own dimensions, count of modern catalogue copies and generic animal picture are not such referents.

Predeclare the competing predictions. A quantity model predicts a stable count/magnitude relation, ideally proportional or additive with a common unit across more than one context. A nominal category-only model has no such prediction. Rank can predict monotonicity, so monotonic correlation alone still leaves rank unresolved. Arithmetic consistency across independently linked sets, including held-back cases, is much more discriminating than resemblance to tallies.

An unrestricted category model can always encode numerical values as category names. Therefore even a successful test supports the explanatory economy and predictive scope of a quantitative model; it is not a mathematical proof of ancient intention. An independent bilingual reading would supply a different, stronger semantic anchor but is not assumed available.

## Stop rules and concrete deliverable

Produce one object-face eligibility ledger, one photograph/counterexample ledger, prespecified statistics and controls, and one of these decisions:

- **No usable independent variation:** stop internal model escalation; acquire the specific missing observations.
- **No recoverable signal at current sample size:** report insufficient power, not non-numerical meaning.
- **Ordered relation survives controls:** retain an ordered-field hypothesis and pursue independent magnitude evidence; do not claim quantity deciphered.
- **Relation vanishes with deduplication/context control:** reject that association as evidence for a general ordered field.
- **Independent proportional/additive prediction succeeds:** advance the narrow quantitative interpretation, keeping units and language unknown unless separately anchored.

## Priority acquisition gate: three inscribed pots measured in2009

The coordinator reports a lead in Wells2015 chapter4, Table4.1 around printed61: measurements of three inscribed pots at PuranaQila, including H-372, reportedly circumference, inside height and mouth width. This review has **not independently inspected that table or its photographs**. Treat these details as an acquisition lead until the original pages are obtained. This lead takes priority over additional text-only semantic modelling because it could provide an external physical magnitude.

### Required source acquisition before calculation

- Obtain the full table, caption, method discussion, associated profiles/photos, and preceding/following explanation; record printed and PDF pages.
- Link each table row to a specific physical pot and inscription. Determine whether modern reconstruction, missing base/rim, estimated height, or catalogue aliases are involved. Do not assume all three are independent finds merely because they are separate table rows.
- Preserve measurements exactly as published with units and uncertainty, including whether circumference is maximum girth or mouth circumference and whether height is inside or outside. Determine whether actual capacity was measured, reconstructed, or calculated by the author.
- Two readers independently count only clearly preserved inscription strokes before examining the capacity comparison. Preserve uncertain alternative readings. A cropped/truncated photograph cannot establish the total tally: obtain an adequate view or exclude that pot from the primary calculation.
- Require at least two different confirmed tally values. Three pots with one tally value do not test proportional scaling with count.

### What three dimensions do and do not permit

Circumference, mouth width and height generally do not identify the volume of a bulging vessel: many interior profiles share those measurements. If measured liquid capacity is available, prefer it. If a calibrated complete interior profile exists, integrate its cross-sectional area and propagate digitization and reconstruction uncertainty. A cylinder/cone/frustum approximation is a declared geometric assumption and must not be presented as measured capacity. Exterior profiles additionally require wall-thickness information. Without sufficient geometry, report a feasibility failure rather than a spurious precise volume.

### Prespecified three-object calculation

For each eligible pot obtain an independently justified capacity interval V_i and observed tally k_i with alternatives where uncertain. Examine q_i=V_i/k_i. Under the narrow hypothesis **capacity equals a common unit times literal stroke count**, the uncertainty intervals for q_i should have a common intersection. Report the actual ratios and intervals, not a correlation headline. If defensible measurement-error distributions are unavailable, use sensitivity ranges rather than invented statistical confidence intervals.

If intervals have no common overlap over justified geometric/reading alternatives, this contradicts that narrow common-unit proportional model for these pots. It does not refute all quantity interpretations: the marks could refer to contents below full capacity, a different measurement, or unrelated quantities. Do not rescue the specific model by inventing pot-specific conversion factors after the result.

If intervals overlap, report compatibility only. With three selected examples a category/rank model can fit the same pattern, and model selection cannot establish numerical semantics. Permuting three distinct count labels yields only six assignments; such a toy comparison is descriptive and cannot provide a conventional one-sided p<.05 result. If values repeat, there are even fewer distinct assignments. Selection of the illustrated pots may already favour the proposed relation.

For a limited predictive check, estimate a common unit on two objects and predict the third without refitting, cycling which object is left out. This is transparent leave-one-out compatibility, not independent validation: all three selected pots are already known. Record whether the predicted interval covers the observed third capacity. No arbitrary tolerance should be chosen after looking at the ratios.

### Decision and next observation

- **Sources/geometry inadequate:** list exactly which measurements or views would make the experiment possible.
- **Proportional model incompatible:** retain the counterexample and stop presenting these pots as evidence for that rule.
- **Compatible within narrow uncertainty:** freeze the inferred unit/rule and seek a fourth independently selected, securely linked pot with a different tally and measurable capacity.
- **Only compatible under very broad geometry intervals:** evidence is uninformative, even if the intervals overlap.

The strongest next result is a source-checked three-pot measurement table with honest geometric uncertainty, followed by a falsifiable prediction for an independently selected object. Three selected illustrations cannot distinguish quantity from arbitrary categories by themselves.

The next immediate action is acquiring and independently checking the Wells measurement pages. The64-stem eligibility ledger remains a secondary structural experiment; it must not displace this possible external-magnitude test.
