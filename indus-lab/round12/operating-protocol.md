# Autonomous research protocol

Decision owner: research lead. The user need not choose statistical methods or interpretations. Agents supply different kinds of evidence; agreement among agents is not independent archaeological evidence.

## Roles and gates

1. Evidence curator links inscriptions to photographed faces, objects, excavation identifiers, dates and physical measurements. Preserve untouched source files and source hashes. Record unknowns rather than guessing.
2. Literature reviewer checks whether the proposed finding is already published and separates an author's measurement from an author's interpretation.
3. Experiment designer states competing explanations, an observable prediction, exclusions, dependency groups, uncertainty and a failure criterion before fitting.
4. Implementer executes that specification with a finite declared budget and an inclusion ledger. Log deviations; never overwrite failed results.
5. Adversarial reviewer checks copying, aliasing, missingness, sign coding, geometry, leakage and alternate explanations. A blocked gate is a result, not an invitation to search for a smaller p-value.
6. Lead chooses the next action by whether it can distinguish the remaining explanations. Escalate computation only when more computation changes the evidential decision.

## Evidence ladder

E0: supplied record. E1: image-checked observation. E2: reproducible structural association. E3: independently supported function. E4: reading with new predictions. These are per-claim labels, not a percentage deciphered.

## Current exposure and identity

All existing corpus data are retrospectively exposed. A fresh random split cannot turn them into untouched evidence. A new confirmation set needs a frozen protocol, an exposure log and separation by physical-object/production/alias dependencies. Blinded recoding of old pictures validates measurement, not prediction on new inscriptions.

Source ID stems are provisional object groups, not verified physical identities. Preserve every supplied face, distinguish catalogue suffixes from photographic face letters, and keep aliases unresolved until checked. Raw object labels and reviewed corrections remain separate layers.

## Selected next experiment

Acquire and inspect Wells 2015 Table 4.1 (printed page 61), Figure 4.6 (60), and the measurement method for H-370/H-371/H-372. These are acquisition targets, not verified numeric inputs. The official publisher sample confirms the table and figure exist but does not supply their cells.

Extract measured dimensions separately from calculated volumes. Circumference, mouth width and height alone do not uniquely determine a bulging pot's capacity. Prefer direct capacity measurements or calibrated interior profiles; otherwise explicitly justify geometry and uncertainty bounds. Do not estimate object scale from a proposed sign reading. Do not use tablet size as the quantity written on it.

Under the narrow model V = k*u, independently bound each capacity V and read each stroke count k from photographs. Compare intervals for u = V/k. Non-overlap rejects this narrow model; overlap is compatibility, not decipherment. Three selected vessels cannot validate a broad system. Freeze the rule and uncertainty before testing an additional independently measured vessel.

If measurements cannot be acquired lawfully through available sources, retain the blocked gate and pursue acquisition of independent physical context. No fabricated table values, linguistic labels, or unlimited-compute promises.
