# Round 31 — Transcription check of the 8 foreign round seals used in round 30

11 October 2026.

Sources consulted:
- **Gadd 1932**, "Seals of Ancient Indian Style found at Ur", PBA 18 (archive.org item `in.gov.ignca.33779`). Plates I and III and pp. 12–14 inspected as page images.
- **Mahadevan 1977**, *The Indus Script: Texts, Concordance and Tables*, MASI 77 (archive.org). West Asian texts on p. 160; Appendix V on p. 829.
- **Laursen 2010**, AAE 21:96–134. Read as extracted TEXT only; the figure pages were not viewable here.

Not reached: British Museum high-resolution photos (the browser needed an approval for every action), Al-Sindi 1999, Kjærum 1994, Amiet 1973.

**Caveat.** The ICIT/corpus readings may derive from Mahadevan. Where they agree, that is consistent lineage, not independent verification. Only Gadd's photographs and Laursen's prose are independent of the corpus, and Gadd's 1932 halftones are too coarse to resolve stroke numerals.

## Other identity findings
- Gadd no. 17 (BM 120228) is not from Ur. Gadd: "no information as to its origin". It is Mahadevan 9901 ("prob. W. Asia") and was not in the round-30 group.
- Gadd 1932 no. 15 = U.8685 = corpus 3899.1 (contains 000) and was not in the test. It is Laursen no. 20 (grave PG 401), which Laursen describes as having a "man" sign, a crab variant, a scorpion and a footprint.
