## Remaining limits

- **Comb teeth:** the reference has six horizontal arm levels, counting the top and bottom. The M-478 comb appears compatible at coarse scale, but shadows, a crowded upper boundary, and small size prevent a secure exact count. Its stem/arm orientation is reversed relative to the reference.
- **Orientation:** comparing a reflected comb to the reference is reasonable for morphology. A global reflection also reverses the spatial order of the four units. Therefore, glyph reflection and sequence direction should not be silently conflated, and no reading direction follows from this comparison.
- **Figure boundaries:** the proposed inclusion of both flanking uprights in 097 is visually plausible and substantially reduces the apparent token count. Their precise separation from adjacent shapes is still affected by small scale and relief shadows.
- **Grouped uprights:** a short-stroke group is clear after the U-like unit. Exactly which narrow marks belong to it, rather than to the neighboring U or outer motif, is less secure; an exact four-stroke certification would overstate this scan. The rendered 004 reference also includes dotted marks above and below; those are not independently identifiable as corresponding photographic features here, and their rendering convention was not investigated.
- **Scope:** the local match supports a four-unit candidate on the photographed A face. It does not independently verify the entire database record, catalogue crosswalk, normalization conventions, or the classification of the flanking motifs.
