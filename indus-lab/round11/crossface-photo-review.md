## A-face comparison

All three A photographs show a comparable broad three-part arrangement, in the same spatial order in their uppercase views:

1. At left, a narrow upright form with several short lateral projections, broadly comb-like or branched.
2. Centrally, a taller narrow U-like form, with two long sides approaching or joining below. Short upper details are less consistently resolved.
3. At right, a pointed or narrow loop-like upper body attached to diverging lower strokes.

H-315 A is small and its close-set forms make some junctions hard to isolate. H-316 A shows the three groups more distinctly; its lower-case a view places their counterparts in reversed order. H-317 A also supports the three-part arrangement, while A bis is more worn or broken and is less useful for fine details.

**A-face conclusion:** yes, the photographs support the same three broad glyph structures across H-315 A, H-316 A, and H-317 A. They do not establish identity of every tooth, minor projection, interior mark, or stroke junction. These coarse similarities can therefore be stated alongside the visibly differing B-face stroke counts without treating a full normalized transcription as photographically verified.
