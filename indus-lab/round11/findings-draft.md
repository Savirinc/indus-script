## Fixed text and variable stroke field across faces

The raw catalogue joins text rows by ID stem. Retaining simple complete rows without000/999 and examining all recorded faces, 27 distinct other-face text sets occur with at least two different long-stroke codes adjacent to ICIT700. All 27 observed families are from Harappa. This is descriptive extraction, not an independence-adjusted frequency claim or newly discovered ancient function.

Concrete examples:

Their raw excavation identifiers differ:10185c,2480,3025. Physical identity and exact drawings require catalogue inspection. The larger400–740–176 family has33quantity rows but32ID stems; H-355 contributes two identical quantity-side rows. Do not count33independent objects.

A stricter extraction requiring every recorded row of each ID stem to be complete/simple produces the same27families. It still cannot prove unrecorded faces absent or catalogue identifications correct. Exact duplicate texts and mold-family copies do not provide independent repetitions.

The narrow inference is a stable text field plus a variable stroke field. Quantity is a plausible interpretation consistent with existing scholarship; specific goods, units, names, payments, ritual offerings, and language are unestablished.
