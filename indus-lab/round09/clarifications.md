# Post-run clarifications — frozen protocol left unchanged
The protocol sentence 'All faces represented in text are retained' refers only to signs inside the selected representative text row. Separate face rows in a duplicate-connected component are NOT concatenated: one lexicographically selected ID row represents that component. Eligibility is assessed per row before grouping. Consequently other faces, short texts, damaged texts and shared cross-site sign multisets may be absent. The population is selected site-specific analytical groups, not all Indus objects.
Subtypes reported in diagnostics.json are original catalogue labels, so one corrected Harappa tablet is still shown as raw SEAL:R. The target uses the reviewed correction.
The diagnostic subtype and individual sign counts were inspected after results and are exploratory. They are not new confirmatory tests. Neither coefficient rankings nor sign400 counts establish meaning.
Both city datasets were used as training and test in opposite transfers: held-out refers to each independently fitted model, not a permanently untouched final validation corpus. A future revised model must be assessed on new held-out evidence.
