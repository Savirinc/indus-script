# Independent photographic review

The reviewer inspected both volume-I plates, the replacement original-scan volume-II plates and the modern font sample. The photographs do not establish the claimed presence/absence split.

M-527 A has a plausible short isolated stroke near the left end. M-528–530 are less decisive. M-1486 A bis also has a possible short mark immediately left of the first larger curved form, with uncertain separation from neighbouring strokes. None of M-1482–1486 or M-1488 provides sufficiently secure negative evidence to certify absence across that group.

The clearer reverses share an elephant-like outline and a small angular, barred or branching accessory below/in front of the trunk. Preservation obscures details. No consistent accessory difference tracks the proposed text groups, but exact accessory equivalence is also unresolved.

Decision: keep the extra-stroke distinction unresolved. A bis image is another view of the same object, not an additional tablet.
