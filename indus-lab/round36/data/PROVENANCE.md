# PROVENANCE — rebus_data (built 2026-10-12)

Everything was produced by `build_rebus.py`, which is in this folder. Run it with `python3 -I build_rebus.py`. No forms were typed by hand. Every row in a `lang_*.tsv` points to a source row ID. The one hand-curated table is `chinese_pictographs.tsv`, where the choice of character for each concept is the compiler's; its readings are all pulled from the sources below.

## Method notes

* **Concept mapping.** For CLDF/lexibank datasets (IDS, WOLD, ASJP, sagartst), concepts were mapped by Concepticon ID only; the ID sets are in `build_rebus.py`. The wider IDs in use are: MOUNTAIN OR HILL 2118, FISHNET 388, CATTLE 1153 (counted as bull/ox), FLOWING BODY OF WATER 3311 (river), TORTOISE 895, BANYAN 346, and PERSON 683 / MALE 1553 (counted as man). A dataset parameter without a Concepticon ID was matched on the first sense of its gloss; the notes column says "no Concepticon ID".
* **Raw dictionaries.** The Dravidian DB, JAMBU Munda files and Yoshioka have no Concepticon IDs. They were matched by exact-segment gloss matching: the gloss is split on `, ; /`, parentheses and articles are stripped, and a segment must equal one of the concept's English keywords. For JAMBU sources only the first gloss sense counts. Matches are not semantically verified, so check them before scoring.
* **PDr selection.** The candidates are rows with `source_classification == "Proto-Dravidian"` whose meaning matches. They are ranked with Krishnamurti 2003 first, then a first-sense match, then DEDR number. The top row is noted `PRIMARY (standard reconstruction)` and up to 3 alternatives are kept. Rhinoceros has no PDr match, and the Tamil DEDR fallback found nothing either, so it stays blank.
* **Multiple forms.** There are up to 4 forms per concept per source, and later ones are marked "alternative". ASJP rows are appended last and marked "ASJP supplement".
* **`form_ipa_or_simplified`.** Diacritics are dropped. Retroflexes become T D N L R S (and a dot-below on c/j/z is upper-cased). Long vowels are doubled, from a macron or ː. ŋ→ng, ɲ/ñ→ny, ś/ʃ/š→sh, ḻ/ẓ/ɻ→zh, ɟ→j, ə/ɛ→e, ɔ→o, glottal stop ʔ/ˀ→?, and hyphens are removed. Parenthesised optional segments are dropped and only the first variant before `, ; / ~` is kept.
  * Old Chinese uses the Baxter–Sagart notation as published, with `ˤ` (pharyngealisation) dropped and `C.` kept as `c`.
  * ASJP rows are simplified from the CLDF Segments (IPA), not from ASJPcode. ASJP does not mark retroflexion.
  * IDS Finnish uses the IDS phonemic orthography (w for v, æ, ü); Elamite IDS forms are syllabic transliterations (e.g. na-h-hu-un-te).
  * One Kharia form (elephant, केलुंग) is in Devanagari only, so its simplified cell is empty.
* **Chinese corpus.** `chinese_corpus.txt` has one text per line, CJK characters only, punctuation stripped. The 1,562 lines are Shijing 305 poems, Lunyu paragraphs, Mengzi, Daxue and Zhongyong. **Shijing and Lunyu are in simplified characters as published by chinese-poetry**; Mengzi, Daxue and Zhongyong are traditional. `chinese_corpus_trad.txt` converts the simplified lines with Unihan kTraditionalVariant. Where a character has several candidates, the first candidate (or the character itself) is used. There are 281 such characters, 5,293 tokens in all, listed in `chinese_corpus_trad_ambiguous.tsv`, so check them before use. Line provenance is in `chinese_corpus_index.tsv`.
* **chinese_chars.tsv.** It holds 5,923 characters: those with kGradeLevel (HK school list) or kHanyuPinlu, plus every corpus character. Rows are sorted by corpus frequency, then Hanyu Pinlu frequency. Columns: Mandarin, Cantonese, Tang (kTang, sparse), MC (PHONO-ML Baxter), Unihan fanqie, OC (Baxter–Sagart, only from the sagartst HANZI field: 263 characters), kPhonetic (Casey phonetic classes), grade, frequencies and definition. **Per-character Baxter–Sagart OC for the full set was NOT obtainable.** The BS 2014 list on ocbaxtersagart.lsait.lsa.umich.edu and its Wayback copies could not be reached from the shell.
* **chinese_phonetic_series.tsv.** This is the 諧聲 proxy from Unihan kPhonetic (Casey 1980 phonetic classes): 2,919 classes covering all Unihan characters that have the field.
* **chinese_pictographs.tsv.** The concept→character choice (象形 graphs, with indicative/loan numerals flagged) is the compiler's selection from the standard Shuowen-tradition classification, and disputed identifications are flagged in notes. Readings come from the sources above. 36 of the 68 graphs have BS OC; the rest have MC and Mandarin only.
