# Sources - Gulf/Indus personal names in cuneiform (c. 2400-1600 BCE, later items date-flagged)

## Primary corpus (verified directly)
- CDLI full ATF dump + catalogue (cdli-gh/data on GitHub: cdliatf_unblocked.atf, cdli_cat.csv), downloaded 2026-10-11. All P-numbers, line readings, periods and year-dates in the TSV for CDLI items come from these files (grep for me-luh-ha, ma2-gan, dilmun/tilmun, NI.TUK -> 1,469 lines in 859 texts, hand-filtered for person contexts).
  - Key texts: Nisaba 15, 371 (P453801); Nisaba 15, 951 (P454137, anonymous "lu2 me-luh-ha-me"); CT 50, 76 (P212982); CTMMA 1, 17 (P108701); PPAC 5, 746 (P378016); TUT 154 (P135727); RIME 2.01.04.13 (P216559); VS 14, 30/194, RTC 26, ASJ 19, 54 (Presargonic Dilmun copper trade); RTC 337 (P128490); UET 5, 367 (P283686); VS 27, 98 (P020317 - anonymous "lady of Dilmun" sending gifts to the queen of Lagash).
  - Anonymous Gulf persons (no names): lu2 ma2-gan{ki} in PPAC 5, 59 (P315657), Babyloniaca 8 Pupil 17 (P104826), AfO 24 Truro 2 (P100288); lu2 me-luh-ha-me in Nisaba 15, 951.
- cdli.earth web is reachable (200) but its search endpoint returned errors; ORACC was unreachable from the shell.

## Secondary (read via web; not checked against the original publications)
- Laursen, S. & Steinkeller, P. 2017. *Babylonia, the Gulf Region and the Indus* (MC 21), pp. 83-84 - Nanaza/Samar as the only possible evidence of the Meluhhan language; quoted via https://www.harappa.com/blog/only-known-meluhan-personal-names-samar-and-nanaza
- Marchesi, G. 2024. "The cuneiform texts from Failaka", in *Failaka/Dilmun: The Second Millennium Settlements* 6, pp. 75-112 - CFa seal/object numbers, readings, language classes. https://www.academia.edu/125777568/The_cuneiform_texts_from_Failaka
- Marchesi, G. 2017. "Inscriptions from the Royal Mounds of A'ali", in Laursen, *The Royal Mounds of A'ali in Bahrain*, pp. 425-430 (Rimum, Yagli-El, Sumu-lel) - via https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yagli-El
- Laursen, Al Shaikh, Reeler, Lombard & Marchesi 2026, JNES 85/1 - inscribed stamped tokens from Bahrain ("servant of Panipa, Yagli-El") - via Wikipedia; the article itself was not reached (GDN pages blocked/captcha).
- Potts & Blau 1998; Zadok 2018; al-Nashef 2012; Dalley 2009/2013; Glassner 2009 - Inzak-theophoric names at Susa, Ur, Lagaba, Sealand - via https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inzak (UNVERIFIED primary).
- Dilmun article (Ili-ippasra, Sumu-lel, Samu'a): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dilmun
- Parpola, Parpola & Brunswig 1977, JESHO 20 ("The Meluhha village") - interpretive background for the Lagash Meluhhans (not re-read).

## Not reached / gaps
- Potts, *The Arabian Gulf in Antiquity*; Glassner and Zadok onomastic studies; Hojlund; Eidem & Hojlund Failaka; Steinkeller 2006 (Marhasi) - not accessed in full.
- Mari archive (Durand/Charpin) - no named Dilmunite found in this pass.
- Isin craft archive "Dilmun" entries (BIN 9/10) - only BIN 10, 129 (Dilmun boat) checked; no Dilmunite names found.
- Kassite Qal'at al-Bahrain tablets (Andre-Salvini & Lombard; Cavigneaux) - not mined.
- Cuneiform-inscribed Gulf seals from Ur/Susa not individually checked.
