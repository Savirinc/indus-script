Native scan extraction and independent visual review of M-1543–M-1547, printed page 226 / PDF page 261. This is a targeted difficult page, not a representative test of the whole volume.

Result: 10 exact-pixel crops and 10 face records checked. Both reviewers abstained from complete transcription on every face. One object matches the existing CSV; four remain unmatched. No new reading or meaning is claimed.

Display enlargement adds no source detail. Source page: 805 × 1132 pixels, approximately 100 ppi. Images are unenhanced; crops preserve the decoded source pixels. Descriptions concern photograph visibility, not proof that a surface originally lacked marks.

M-1543-A

Native crop 276 × 204 px · as published

Complete transcription: unresolved

Reviewer A

Dark, roughly rectangular face with dense mottling and fine surface striations. Scattered short linear and curved traces are faintly visible, especially toward the lower portion, but do not form a securely traceable row.

Low contrast and surface texture prevent reliable identification of all intentional strokes, their boundaries, or their order. An unreadable sequence is not evidence of an empty face.

Reviewer B

Broad rectangular face with rounded corners and very dark, mottled surface reproduction. Scattered pale speckles and faint irregular linear or curved traces; no securely separable complete row of marks.

Abstain. At the supplied native image resolution, surface texture and possible grooves cannot be reliably separated across the whole face. This is unknown content, not an observation of an empty face.

Complete transcription: unresolved

Reviewer A

Broken rectangular fragment with conspicuous bright jagged lines crossing the upper portion and descending near the right side. Finer upright and curved traces appear within the remaining surface, especially around the middle and lower-left, but are interrupted.

Fracture-like lines and incomplete fine traces cannot be cleanly separated throughout the face. The preserved image does not support a complete transcription.

M-1547-A

Native crop 262 × 137 px · as published

Complete transcription: unresolved

Reviewer A

Elongated rectangular face with faint linear marks across its lower-middle region. A strong dark diagonal fracture-like feature descends from the upper edge through the central area. A small group of short uprights is visible right of center, with weaker curved and angular traces nearby and farther left.

The central interruption and weak surrounding marks prevent a complete position-by-position tracing. A few local features are visible, but missing units must not be supplied from comparisons.

Reviewer B

Elongated rectangular face with a prominent dark irregular diagonal line descending from near the upper middle. A shallow curved trace and several short approximately vertical traces appear around the lower-middle/right area. An isolated short pale vertical trace appears farther left; a bright patch occurs at the upper-right corner.

Abstain. This face offers several more recognizable local traces, but their full boundaries and relationships remain uncertain. The diagonal discontinuity and low contrast prevent a confident complete sequence.

Elongated face with a narrow upright, partly enclosed or U-like outline near the center and short crosswise or oblique marks within or beside it. A broad dark vertical band overlaps this area. Additional weak curved or upright traces occur to the right.

Abstain. The central discontinuity appears compatible with damage, while adjacent potential grooves are incomplete or poorly separated from surface texture. Full-sequence recovery is not defensible at this resolution.
