All seven positive Mohenjo-daro representatives now have located plate images, but they are not all photographically verified transcriptions. Negative controls and sign-present square seals remain to be checked. The independent morphology review agrees on broad shapes in the first five objects while explicitly withholding exact sign identity and whole-row validation. This keeps the statistical result at transcription-level exploratory association.
