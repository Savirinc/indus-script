# Round 53b — Multi-sign words or one sign per word, with a lexicon-induction segmenter (pre-registered)

Written 19 October 2026 after round 53's METHOD-FAIL, before `run53b.py` exists and before the new segmenter has been run on any corpus. Committed before code.

## What changes from round 53
Only the segmenter. Round 53's pairwise PMI needs pair counts that an 8,726-token corpus cannot supply. A lexicon-induction model scores whole segmentations: every recurrence of a candidate word anywhere in the sample counts for it. The question, corpora, units, gold boundaries, sampling, statistics and verdict rule are those of round 53 (`../round53/PROTOCOL.md`), with two stated changes: B = 30 samples per control corpus instead of 100 (compute), and no τ calibration, because the segmenter has no threshold; its hyperparameters are fixed here instead.

## Segmenter (fixed before running)
Dirichlet-process unigram word model (Goldwater, Griffiths and Johnson 2009), fitted by Gibbs sampling over boundary variables:
- A segmentation of the sample is a set of boundaries between adjacent signs inside units; unit edges are always boundaries.
- Word probability under the model: P(w) = ( n(w) + α0 · P0(w) ) / ( n + α0 ), with n(w) the count of word w in the current segmentation excluding the position being resampled and n the total word count.
- Base distribution P0(w) = p# (1 − p#)^(|w|−1) · Π P(sign), with P(sign) the unigram sign frequencies of the sample and p# = 0.5.
- α0 = 20.
- Boundary at position i is resampled from the ratio of the probabilities of the two local configurations (one word versus two), in the standard way, with simulated annealing: temperature falls linearly from 2 to 1 over the first half of the sweeps.
- 150 sweeps over all internal positions in random order; initial boundaries placed independently with probability 0.5; seed 53101 + corpus index × 1000 + sample index; the segmentation after the last sweep is the output.

## Positive control, statistics, verdict
As round 53: at least three of the four gold corpora (SEAL, SUX, OBL, OAL) must show boundary F1 at least 0.15 above random boundaries placed at the same rate, else METHOD-FAIL. S (mean segment length) and M (share of units with a multi-sign segment) on 30 samples per control and once on the Indus corpus; verdict words MULTI-SIGN-WORDS, SINGLE-SIGN-WORDS, BETWEEN, METHOD-FAIL by the round 53 rule; S and M must agree. Negative-control diagnostic unchanged: Indus shuffled within texts must fall to the CHN level or below.

## Additional reported items
- The induced Indus lexicon: the twenty most frequent multi-sign segments with counts, and their share of tokens. These are descriptive.
- Precision and recall separately, since a lexicon model that under-segments has high precision and low recall.

## Confounds
As round 53 (hapax-rich corpora, unit length, approximate CHN gold). One more: the DP unigram model is known to under-segment (it finds collocations), which should make S higher than gold on every corpus; the comparison is relative, with controls under the same bias.
